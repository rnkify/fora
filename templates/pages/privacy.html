Information you provide

When you submit a contact or project form, Fora receives the details you enter, such as your name, email address, company, website, service interest, and message.

How it is used

Submitted information is used to respond to your inquiry, assess potential work, manage the resulting business relationship, and operate the service. Basic referral and campaign details may be recorded to understand how inquiries reach Fora.

Service providers and retention

Infrastructure and email providers may process information only as needed to operate the website and communications. Records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for business operations, legal obligations, and security.

Questions

For a privacy question or request concerning information you submitted, use the contact form.
